GPU clusters · Slurm & Kubernetes · measured versus asserted

GPU scheduling and fleet health: what was measured, and on what.

I'm Zhanyl Abdybaeva. I build the systems that allocate scarce, expensive, heterogeneous compute, and the benchmarks that check whether they work.

The public work covers three things: controlled evaluation of GPU schedulers across Slurm and Kubernetes (including Slurm on Kubernetes via Slinky), GPU fleet-health automation that refuses to act on missing evidence, and a habit of publishing what was measured separately from what was asserted, negative results and corrections included. Every project page states what is built, partial or planned.

Books that shaped how I think about systems, markets, and engineering. Updated quarterly.

## Systems & Engineering

**High Output Management** — Andy Grove
Treating organizations like engineering pipelines. The management book that reads like a systems design doc. Essential for anyone who manages infrastructure or people.

**Designing Machine Learning Systems** — Chip Huyen
The production ML bible. If you're deploying models, not just training them, start here.

**AI Engineering** — Chip Huyen
The 2025 follow-up. Covers the full stack from inference to deployment in the era of foundation models.

## Quantitative Finance

**The Man Who Solved the Market** — Gregory Zuckerman
How Jim Simons and Renaissance Technologies proved that elite scientists and HPC engineers could systematically extract alpha from financial markets. The history of what you're building toward.

## Decision-Making & Strategy

**Thinking in Bets** — Annie Duke
Professional poker player on decision-making under uncertainty. Directly applicable to risk management, trading decisions, and engineering tradeoffs.
